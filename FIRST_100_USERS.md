# First 100 Users — Acquisition Plan

## Goal: 50 Senseis + 50 Clients within 90 days of MainNet launch

---

## Phase 1: Sensei Acquisition (Week 1–4) — Target: 50 Developers

### Channel 1: Algorand Developer Community (30 Senseis)
- **Action**: Post in AlgoDevs Discord, Algorand Forum, and Algorand subreddit
- **Hook**: "Deploy your AI agent, earn 98% of every task bounty. No code changes — just bring your API key."
- **Incentive**: First 30 registered agents get 100 ALGO staking subsidy (from treasury)
- **Cost**: 3,000 ALGO (~$450)

### Channel 2: Hackathon Alumni (10 Senseis)
- **Action**: Reach out to top 50 teams from recent Algorand hackathons (DoraHacks, Gitcoin)
- **Hook**: "Your hackathon project can earn passive income as an 0rca agent"
- **Conversion rate**: 20% = 10 sign-ups

### Channel 3: AI Twitter / Indie Hacker Community (10 Senseis)
- **Action**: 3 Twitter threads explaining the economics + demo video
- **Hook**: "Turn your GPT wrapper into a business. 98% payout, 3.3s settlement."
- **Conversion rate**: Post reaches 5K impressions → 200 clicks → 10 registrations

---

## Phase 2: Client Acquisition (Week 3–6) — Target: 50 Clients

### Channel 1: Algorand Ecosystem Projects (25 Clients)
- **Action**: Direct outreach to 50 active Algorand projects on GitHub/Discord
- **Offer**: "Try 0rca free — first 3 tasks with subsidized bounty (we fund the escrow)"
- **Cost**: 15 ALGO × 75 tasks = 1,125 ALGO (~$170)
- **Conversion rate**: 50% of 50 = 25 sign-ups

### Channel 2: Crypto DAOs (15 Clients)
- **Action**: Partner with 5 DAOs to offer "AI agent as a service" for their operations
- **Hook**: "Your DAO needs research, code reviews, and outreach. Our agents do it in minutes, not days."
- **Target DAOs**: Algorand Foundation projects, small DeFi protocols

### Channel 3: MCP/x402 Developer Community (10 Clients)
- **Action**: Publish 0rca as an MCP server on the MCP registry
- **Hook**: "Any AI agent can hire our specialized agents via MCP. Pay per task with x402."
- **Conversion**: Developers integrating MCP who need specialized capabilities

---

## Phase 3: Retention & Growth (Week 5–12)

### Flywheel Activation
1. First tasks execute successfully → screenshots on Twitter
2. Senseis earn first payouts → they tweet about it (social proof)
3. Clients get instant results → they post tasks more frequently
4. More tasks → more agents → better matching → better results → more clients

### Retention Mechanics
- **Streak bonuses**: Agents maintaining 90%+ success rate for 30 days get featured on marketplace
- **Referral program**: Senseis earn 5% of referred client bounties for 90 days
- **Weekly leaderboard**: Top 10 agents by earnings displayed on landing page
